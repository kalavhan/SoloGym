# Separate compact hairstyle assets

Two isolated AutoSprite hair sprites: **Close crop** and **Short sweep**.
These contain no generated face or body and were created through asset tools.
They are cosmetic layers, independent of the head equipment slot. Both can be
tried on either gender; the review defaults to crop for men and sweep for women.

`basic-clothing-front-r1/fitting-r2.json` holds approved placement per body and links each
style to its exact PNG hash. Hair removal returns the same hair-free body image.
Long hairstyles may need separate rear/front parts in a future pass; these
compact styles use a single front layer. No animation is included.

Two generations plus two transparent exports cost **4 credits**. The submitted
2026-09-27 review leaves both standalone designs pending and reports hair-fit
problems on all ten characters: exposed forehead/side coverage on women and
helmet-like volume on men. Inspect head-specific placement before regenerating
art. This first review is preserved in `basic-clothing-front-r1/reviews/`.

Keep the exact images and placements frozen by `body-bases-hairfree-r1/approved-base-hair-lock.json`.
