# Fasting stage copy — evidence checked 2026-10-01

The clock measures time since a user-entered meal/end-of-eating time. It does
not measure glucose, ketones, autophagic flux, hormones or immune function.
The art may change by elapsed-time range; health claims must not be inferred
from that decorative change.

## Why the submitted five-stage copy needs adjustment

- **Ketosis:** a correction to the NEJM review describes rising ketones over
  time rather than a universal binary switch. It does not establish that every
  user is in “full ketosis” at 16 hours. The interface therefore avoids naming
  a timer interval as a measured metabolic state.
  [NEJM correction](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMx200002).
- **Autophagy:** a 2025 exploratory analysis in 121 adults with obesity assessed
  a blood-cell measure over months. A post hoc between-group difference appeared
  at six months, without a significant within-intervention increase from
  baseline. This does not establish an acute 16- or 24-hour activation threshold.
  [Journal of Physiology, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40345145/).
- **Immune regeneration:** the cited line of research primarily investigated
  mice, with preliminary human observations during chemotherapy. It is not
  evidence of a universal 72-hour immune-regeneration stage in healthy users.
  [Cheng et al., Cell Stem Cell](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4102383/).
- **Duration:** Johns Hopkins notes that extended 24–72-hour fasts are not
  necessarily better and can be dangerous. Its exclusions include under-18s,
  pregnancy/breastfeeding, type 1 diabetes using insulin and eating-disorder
  history. Keep the existing suitability flow; the app's 20-hour plan cap is
  a scope limit, not medical clearance.
  [Johns Hopkins Medicine](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/expert-qa/intermittent-fasting-what-is-it-and-how-does-it-work).

## Proposed Spanish display

| Elapsed range | Neutral label | Visual distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 h | Tras la comida | Muted brass rune marker |
| 4–16 h | Entre comidas | Muted sage rune marker |
| 16–24 h | Intervalo de 16–24 h | Muted teal rune marker |
| 24–48 h | Intervalo de 24–48 h | Muted blue-gray rune marker |
| 48+ h | Más de 48 h | Quiet slate marker; no intensified effect |

Shared caption: **Etapa orientativa por tiempo**.
Shared explanation: **El reloj no mide cambios metabólicos.**
Show the current range and an optional explanatory guide, not a reward ladder
or countdown to a more desirable stage. These bands include actual/corrected
records outside supported plans; they are not selectable duration goals.

This is product-copy review, not individual medical advice or clinical approval.
