# Illustrated character assets

Read `style-lock.json` and `../../docs/design/character-2-5d-art-direction.md`
before creating or revising character art in this directory.

- The user approved the illustrated style in
  `references/illustrated-style-r1.png`. Preserve that game illustration style,
  linework, hair masses, authored skin shading and material highlights. The
  generic rendered 3D proof is not an accepted appearance reference.
- Preserve one adult identity per presentation across the seven body presets.
  The male anchor is `references/male-kai-identity-r1.png`. The female anchor is
  `references/female-identity-r1.png`, approved through the matching
  `female-normal` r1 source in the user's recorded review.
- The final approval in `approval-r1/reviews/user-approval-all-14.json` approves
  all fourteen currently selected appearances, including `male-chubby` r3,
  `male-fat` r3 and `female-fat` r4. There are no open art corrections. Preserve
  every approved source and hash; keep earlier review exports and the chat
  supplement as history. Future revisions must not alter these masters or
  inherit their decisions. The manifest records the exact selected sources.
- User labels are `skinny`, `normal`, `chubby`, `fat`, `skinny muscular`,
  `muscular`, `fat muscular`. IDs use underscores. `normal` has a softly flat
  abdomen without a carved six-pack. `fat_muscular` combines a substantial soft
  belly and waist with muscular shoulders and limbs. These are appearance
  options, never health labels or inferred fitness measurements.
- Apply the user's project-specific proportion direction: female presets have
  smaller muscle volume than the corresponding male presets. Preserve the
  female identity and proportionate arms and legs; do not amplify limbs into
  oversized bodybuilding shapes. This is an art choice for SoloGym, not a
  claim about universal anatomy or biology.
- The user rejected the amplified `female-fat_muscular` r2 arms and legs as
  oversized. Distinguish that preset through a soft belly/full waist with
  moderate strength cues, not enlarged shoulder, arm or leg bulk. Keep female
  muscular definition restrained as well. Do not repeat the earlier advice
  to add more female muscle volume merely to make preset differences obvious.
- The approved masters incorporate the historical body feedback:
  nonmuscular skinny/chubby arms should read softer, not like the muscular
  presets; male normal is mildly fuller without visible abs; male fat has a
  fuller soft chest and belly; male fat muscular has proportionate arms;
  female fat has softer, slightly slimmer arms and retains a protruding belly;
  female fat muscular has a modestly smaller bust; female muscular has moderate
  muscle while remaining proportionate and smaller than male muscular.
  These are project art directions, not health or biological classifications.
- Completed corrections reduced actual contours, not only shading:
  `male-chubby` arm width, `male-fat` arm width and muscular pectoral forms,
  and `female-fat` thigh/calf bulk while preserving her fat body and belly.
  These achieved rules guide future derived assets; they do not reopen any
  approved source.
- Keep the three review panels: base studio, equipped studio, and equipped
  elevated three-quarter gameplay view. Use the same baseline, nominal height,
  lighting and palette across candidates. Do not individually scale bodies to
  fill their panels. Keep full hair and feet visible.
- Keep the review outfit and hairstyles fixed while comparing bodies. Male:
  Kai's spiky black hair, base charcoal shorts. Female: adult consistent face,
  black high ponytail with spiky flyaways, base charcoal sports bra and shorts.
  Equipped: high-neck black top, dark pants, gloves and boots with cyan and
  silver accents. Use warm medium skin for this initial comparison.
- Start each preset from a coherent full-body master. Do not generate isolated
  production limbs, independent collars or floating face features. Fit clothing
  to its actual master and view; do not stretch another body's outfit to fit.
- Equipment production requires an authored fit for every supported body/view
  combination, sharing that body's registration, pose and frame coordinates.
  These static review boards establish appearance; they are not rig assets or
  interchangeable equipment layers.
- Saved references and approved masters are immutable. New changes get a new
  revision filename. Repeated AI generation is not pixel-deterministic even
  with the same prompt; derive new work from the saved masters and compare it.
- Save exact prompts, reference paths/hashes, output hashes and available
  generation metadata in the batch manifest. Do not invent undisclosed model
  IDs or seeds. A proposed path is not evidence a file has been generated.
- The requested fourteen-board appearance review is complete. Preserve all
  source approvals and female identity approval. Production layers, animation
  and runtime readiness remain unverified; these boards are not interchangeable
  sprites. Record any future approval only when provided, without inferring it
  from silence. Registered exports and runtime fit/motion verification are
  still required before these assets become usable in the game.

This scope covers illustration sources and review artifacts. It does not
authorize unrelated menu, gameplay or runtime changes.
